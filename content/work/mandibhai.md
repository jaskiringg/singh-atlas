# Mandi Bhai

**Transferred to their team.** Freelance — not my startup. I built the early version. I do not operate the current marketplace.

## What I built

Early mobile marketplace and the inventory, pricing and ordering APIs underneath it.

- Shared product layer so the same SKU can be compared across wholesalers
- Cart split per wholesaler before confirmation, minimums visible
- Stock deducted on order placement in that early version
